About us

A small team that answers the phone and shows up.

Comfort Plumbers works plumbing and electrical jobs across Harare and Bulawayo — from a leaking tap to a full installation.

How we work

Comfort Plumbers is run day-to-day with a small, steady team — two regular assistants on every job, with extra hands brought on for bigger installations and let go once the work is complete. That keeps quality consistent, whether it's a same-day repair or a multi-day project.

Every job — big or small — gets a written quotation first: materials priced individually, labour charged separately, and an honest estimate of how long it will take. Nothing starts without your sign-off, and nothing is done for free.

Most of our work comes from clients who call us back for their next property, or refer us to someone else. We try to earn that the same way every time — turn up, quote straight, do the job properly.

What we won't do

We're straightforward about the limits of what we offer — for example, we don't run a honey sucker (septic tank cleaning) service. If it's outside what we do, we'll tell you upfront rather than take the job anyway.
